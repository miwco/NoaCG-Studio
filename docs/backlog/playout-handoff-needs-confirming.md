# Leaving the wizard for the playout client asks nothing

Owner, 2026-09-02, unprompted and flagged by him as **surprisingly important**:

> Today, when you click open in our client the Playout queue, it just goes there without asking
> anything or confirming anything. This is actually one of the reasons why I need a back button,
> because I mistakenly pressed that. It might be that you didn't realize to check the correct
> rundown from the dropdown before you press it, because you think that it will probably ask
> something when you click it, like it does when you click the export button.
>
> It needs to verify: "Are you sure you want to add it to this playout and go there?" or some kind
> of an "Okay" button, so you know that now you're done with the wizard and you will continue to
> the playout.

## Why it matters more than it looks

It is two irreversible-feeling things at once, with no ceremony on either: the graphic is added to
a **rundown chosen in a dropdown he may not have checked**, and the wizard is left behind with no
way back (`back-to-the-wizard.md`). He hit exactly that and had no route home. The EXPORT button
already confirms, which is what set his expectation - the inconsistency is the trap, not the
missing dialog on its own.

## What it needs

A confirmation that names **which rundown** the graphic is going into, so the dropdown he skipped
is stated back to him in the one place he cannot skip. Not a bare "are you sure": the value is in
printing the destination. Model it on the export confirmation's shape so the two read as the same
kind of moment.

Cheaper by half once the wizard can be re-entered, because a wrong answer stops being expensive -
but he asked for the confirmation itself, and it is worth having either way.
