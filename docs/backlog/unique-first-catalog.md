# The catalog leads with unique designs; skins move to the wizard

**Why (owner, 2026-08-28 walk):** the House/Clean/Frost/Volt family matrix fills Browse with
near-identical cards. Families are fine - and a kit switching a whole show's skin is "pretty
smart" - but *"we should just have a unique design, and then in a wizard step you change the
colors and stuff like that... we need to have the first original graphic look unique."*

**Shape:** Browse shows ONE card per design (the unique original); the family/skin variants
resolve behind it - in kits as today, and per-graphic in the wizard's Style step where colors
and look are changed. Depends on the widened family contract (`docs/CATALOG_VARIETY.md` §4.2a)
so families genuinely differ where they still appear.

**Not decided:** how existing family cells map to "one original + styled variants" without
breaking kit resolution or shipped URLs. Needs a plan pass before building.
