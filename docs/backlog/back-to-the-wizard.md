# You cannot get back into the wizard

Raised on the 2026-09-02 walk and expanded the same evening. The wizard draft lives only in React
state, so once a graphic is created there is no route back into the flow that made it - only the
editor, which is a different surface answering different questions.

## What he actually wants, in his own words

> I brought the graphic into the rundown and I noticed that I wanted to change something
> immediately. I pressed the back button on the top left on the screen, I went home and I wanted to
> go and change something in the graphic. It feels like it's impossible to get back to the wizard
> once you have gotten out of it... I want to just redo some animations or something.

> So, that's what I think we should be doing: when you have just imported it to the queue, if you
> press back, you can go back through the last page of the wizard.

> If you're home and you press a graphic and want to edit it, I actually think that we should also
> go back to the wizard if it's made in a wizard, and have all the things that you usually have
> there.

His reasons are all recovery from a decision made one screen too fast: the wrong rundown, an export
he would rather have done differently, an entrance he wants to change from a fade to a fly-in.

## Two entry points, and they are not the same size

1. **Back, straight after creating.** The draft is still in memory; this is "undo the last screen",
   and it is the workflow he actually described. Small.
2. **Open a wizard-made graphic from home.** The draft has to be RECONSTRUCTED from the saved
   template. Much bigger, and it is the one that needs the warning below.

## What he ruled about the editor

> I don't know what the purpose of the editor right now is, because it's like a third editor that
> you can open. I think the wizard is a good place and what most people want to edit. When we have a
> real working editor, then we can also go there, but let's look at that when we have it.

And on hand edits:

> If you have changed your graphic in the editor, then I think you don't need to go back to the
> wizard anymore - but this is when we actually have a working editor. Right now, we do everything
> in the wizard.

> If there's some code or any warnings or something, there could just be a pop-up window that warns
> you that if you go back to the wizard, these things will be reset.

So: re-entering the wizard REGENERATES, it cannot preserve hand-written code, and the honest
answer is a warning that names what will be lost rather than a silent overwrite or a refusal.
