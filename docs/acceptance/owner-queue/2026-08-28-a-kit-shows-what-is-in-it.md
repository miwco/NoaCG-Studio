# A kit shows what is in it

(2026-08-28). Choosing a kit was "buying a pig in a bag" (owner, 2026-08-27): the contents were
33 checkbox labels - "Volt Scorebug", "Slab Bug", "Pager", "Doors Open" - and nothing on the
screen said what any of them looked like. Every row is now a card with a real settled preview of
the design, its name and the graphic type it is a design of. The picking model is unchanged: the
checkboxes still add and drop graphics, and the count is still the number that gets built.

Route, about a minute: **/app -> Start -> Templates -> "A whole kit"**, then click a show. Try
**Match Day** first - it is the biggest at 33 graphics, which is the case that was worst.

What to look at:

- The contents grid under "In this kit" shows each graphic as a picture, not a word. Scroll it;
  previews mount as they come into view, so the step should not stall on arrival.
- Clicking anywhere on a card - the picture included - ticks and unticks it, and the count at
  the top right moves with it.
- Scroll on to **"Add more — everything else this look can build"**. Those cards are dashed
  until ticked, then look exactly like the ones above.
- Change **Look** from `sport` to another family: the pictures should change with it, and your
  ticks should survive the change.
- Type `ticker` in the search box above: rows are hidden, the count stays the whole selection,
  and the line saying how many are hidden by the search still reads true.
