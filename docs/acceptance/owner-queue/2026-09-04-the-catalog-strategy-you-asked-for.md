# The catalog strategy you asked for: what each show runs, and the twelve things the shelf lacks

On 2026-09-03 you said the catalog does not have the width the competition has, that most of
our graphics are "banners with an accent line", and that you wanted research and a strategy
before more drawing. This is that document, not a drawing. Nothing in the product changed.

**Route, about five minutes:** open `docs/CATALOG_BY_PROGRAMME.md` and read §6 first, "The
named absences, ranked". It is twelve items, each with who asks for it, what it says, its
shape and where it sits, and what stands in for it today. Then §5, the genre x kind table,
if you want to see how they were found.

**What to look at.**

- **Do the twelve match what you meant?** The list was built from what talk shows, game
  shows, podcasts, sports, news and film-type shows conventionally put on screen, joined to the
  catalog. Items 1 (the over-the-shoulder box) and 2 (the money ladder, drawn with the question
  board and podiums it sits beside) are proposed as the first two to draw. If your instinct
  says a different one first, that is the one fact the document cannot hold.
- **The measurement under your complaint** (§7): 93% of lower thirds are still one silhouette;
  all twelve quiz boards are one silhouette in one position; nothing in the info-card category,
  which holds every title card and opener, fills the frame; and amber is the largest accent in
  every category measured.
- **The cadence** (§9): weekly, a design row, one named absence closed as a set of three or
  four designs that run a show, proven by the pixel sweep before review, and an owner-queue
  item for you each time. Nightly is proposed for config only (each design's own default
  palette and entrance), not for cards. You said "once a week or each night"; this argues for
  the week and says why.

**What is not in it.** Six genres of sixty formats; esports, corporate, church, commerce,
education and wellness are the next six. Three sources refused to be read and are quoted from
search summaries, and seven lines rest on broadcast convention with no source found; both are
marked in the document itself.
