# Teams design (P1) - ratify to flip ACTIVE

**Ratifying this design is what flips P1 Teams ACTIVE** (`docs/PROGRAMMES.md`); the autumn
class leans on it. Nothing is built yet - this is the plan and the pictures.

The ~5-minute route:

1. Open `docs/TEAMS_PLAN.md`. Read §2 (the model decision: server-authoritative team
   productions, personal libraries untouched) and §8's five owner questions - those five are
   the decisions only you can make.
2. Look at the three mockups (files in `docs/design/teams/`, or the artifacts):
   - Home with a team band: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/2d5384eb-341e-4a87-ba67-96a76045b69d
   - Share dialog / join code / student join: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/e37cd169-0140-46c4-a2ae-e862a3fbec63
   - Team production page (who published, who edited, the concurrent-save notice):
     https://claude.ai/code/artifact/cf733b9a-37b6-40b4-be39-7f2e9480499c
3. What to look at: does the join-code flow (no email) work for how your class actually
   starts? Does "the production moves to the team and leaves your personal list" match how
   you want students to think about ownership? Is the who-did-what visibility enough?

To ratify: say so (answers to §8's questions can come with it or later - v1 defaults are
drafted for each). Ratification + the two sketched migrations is what implementation then
builds; every migration beyond them comes back to you as a scope edge.
