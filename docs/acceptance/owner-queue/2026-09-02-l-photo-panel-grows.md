# A panel you filled with a photo can still grow with the name

Sweep finding 7, and the hole the picture road left behind it. Once a shape filled with a
photograph could be a picture field, it stopped being offered as the panel that grows - so a
full-bleed card, the sort of strap anyone draws, quietly lost its too-long behaviour and the
step offered whatever hairline was left instead. Nobody hit it yet; the road is being proven
now, which is why it is worth closing now.

Route, under a minute: `/app` -> Create -> Import graphic -> drop
`e2e/fixtures/svg-corpus/figma-photo-strap-backplate.svg` (a name strap whose backplate IS the
photograph, with a thin amber tab down its left edge) -> Next.

What to look at:

1. **Both offers, on the one shape.** A **Pictures** row labelled **Strap backplate** - the name
   you gave the strap - and, above it, "when the text is too long" reading **the panel gets
   wider, then the text wraps**, with the line under it naming **Strap backplate** as the shape
   that grows. Before this it read "the text gets smaller", and the only shape on offer was the
   10px tab.
2. **Ticking the picture changes nothing about the growth.** Tick the row and the growth answer
   stays exactly where it was. The two were never a choice, and now they are not made into one.
3. **Both still work on air.** Create project, then in the Content panel type a much longer name
   into **Guest name**: the photo strap widens rather than shrinking the type. Then put a
   picture in the **Strap backplate** field: the strap repaints with it, and clearing the field
   brings your own photograph back.

If the strap widens but the photo does not follow it, or the swap works and the growth stops,
that is the finding - those are the two halves that used to be one marker apart.
